Privacy by design

Mode:

  • No cameras, no faces, no video. Only BLE advertisement packets (ID + RSSI) are observed.
  • Identifiers are salted-hashed and the salt rotates hourly, so IDs cannot be linked across hours.
  • Raw observations are discarded after each short window; only aggregated room-level counts are stored.
  • Even so, wireless IDs can enable tracking if stored carelessly – a real deployment needs consent and policy.
